# RouteBudget — Italian operational-intent research

Date: 6 September 2026. Audience: owner; publication audience: Italian owner-drivers, van operators and small transport businesses.

## Decision and limits

Publish three distinct experiments, not three rewordings of existing cost guides. Select offer screening for vans, delivery-route unit costs, and Italian calendar-to-delivery feasibility. All lead to supported cost-estimation work, not fictitious app functionality. No measured search volume, growth, difficulty or traffic forecast is available. Two exact Italian autocomplete signals and three documented result/source lanes inform selection. Delivery-cost evidence is weaker: a narrow SERP-gap experiment, not a proven popular query.

Current inventory: 62 articles checked before selection, main 0ba6bffe. Original community research log supplied by owner informed the distinction between kilometres, paid work and waiting. It is not a public testimonial or a demand dataset. No participant quotation, identity or contribution from the participant who declined use is published.

## Brief 1 — borsa carichi per furgoni

- Reader: Italian van operator deciding which announcements merit a quote.
- Promise: find compatible work externally; gather missing data; compare two illustrative offers.
- Exclusions: best-platform ranking, subscription prices, promised jobs, platform integrations, truck tariff benchmarks.
- Format: direct answer; first-party search filters; six-field worksheet; two 240 euro offers; decision checklist; truthful app handoff.
- Original calculation: A costs 205.50, B 284; same 240 revenue produces 34.50 and -44.00. A margin 14.375%, displayed 14.38%.
- Intent boundary: not empty-return strategy, generic spot definition, truck subvezione or full N1 cost/km.
- Metadata/title: in the page's meta.json; keyword variations descriptive, not falsely measured individually.
- Pillar/inbound: preventivo-trasporto; contextual incoming from that pillar and costo-furgone-per-km.
- CTA: verify the request, then use supported scenarios/Archive; PDF not order acceptance.

## Brief 2 — costo per consegna ultimo miglio

- Reader: small delivery business pricing a multi-address working day.
- Promise: distinguish stops, successful deliveries and paid units; calculate a route's unit cost and redelivery increment.
- Exclusions: driver salary, universally valid delivery tariff, automatic multi-stop routing, proof of delivery.
- Format: denominator definitions; depot-to-depot cost sheet; 30 attempted/27 successful stops; incremental second attempt; contract questions.
- Original calculation: 23.80+16.80+176+40+10=266.60; 266.60/30=8.8867, /27=9.8741. Revenue 283.50, difference16.90, ratio5.9612%. Redelivery cost4.08+2.88+22=28.96; revenue31.50; difference2.54.
- Intent boundary: distribution unit economics, not general van €/km or pallet pricing.
- Pillar: costi-autotrasporto; contextual incoming from costo-furgone-per-km and preventivo-trasporto.
- CTA: supported component estimate, external stop/cost reconciliation; no counterfeit PDF fields.

## Brief 3 — divieti circolazione mezzi pesanti 2026

- Reader: dispatcher/padroncino checking requested delivery date in Italy.
- Promise: September–December official calendar plus a before-quote feasibility worksheet.
- Exclusions: universal clearance, simplified exemptions, 2027 extrapolation, replacing driver-hours checks.
- Format: official date table; territorial caveat; hypothetical September13 incompatible 08–10 transit; revised-window cost example.
- Original calculation: 35+45+2×24=128; 510+128=638; same700 price leaves62 instead190. Costs and remuneration hypothetical, not statutory.
- Intent boundary: date-to-delivery window, not tachograph or general downtime profitability.
- Pillar/inbound: preventivo-trasporto and tempi-guida-riposo-camion.
- CTA: external compliance check first; app estimates second; no permission generated by PDF.
