# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

European truck drivers, owner-operators, dispatchers, and small transport businesses, with Italy as the first market. They evaluate work under time pressure and need to know whether a proposed trip price protects their margin before accepting it.

## Product Purpose

RouteBudget EU turns route, vehicle, and operating inputs into a clear transport-cost decision. It combines fuel, tolls, driver time, mandatory rest breaks, wear and maintenance, then shows Minimum, Recommended, and Ideal price scenarios. Users can create professional PDF quotes and keep calculations in a local Archive.

Website has two connected jobs. Premium product experience explains value and earns store clicks. Italian-first organic engine answers real transport-cost questions with crawlable guides and limited calculators, then leads naturally to complete app workflow. Success means visitor gets useful answer, understands app boundary, and can reach verified store listing with confidence.

## Organic Growth System

- Italian pillar guides own broad operational problems; supporting guides answer narrower intent.
- Free web calculators expose clear formulas and local-only inputs without reproducing full paid app workflow.
- Every organic page links to pillar, related resources, appropriate app capability, and store action.
- Search Console supplies editorial feedback: indexing, queries, impressions, clicks, CTR, country, and device.
- Proven Italian pages may earn adapted translations at separate locale URLs. Weak pages are not mass-translated.
- Sitemap submission and indexing requests are discovery aids, never traffic or ranking promises.

## Brand Personality

Disciplined, mechanical, assured. Voice is concise, commercial, and factual. Emotional goal: give transport professionals control over a decision that is often made too quickly or by intuition.

## Anti-references

- Never resemble a designer portfolio, creative agency, or personal showreel.
- Never copy Adam Roberts branding, copy, logo, video, awards, or portfolio structure literally.
- Avoid generic SaaS card grids, fake metrics, testimonials, awards, and company logos.
- Avoid gamer neon, cartoon trucks, playful illustration, cheap stock photography, and noisy map dashboards.
- Do not imply turn-by-turn navigation, Android Trip tracking, guaranteed profit, or unsupported features.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with the decision: visitors should understand "know the real price before accepting the trip" immediately.
2. Show operational truth: real app screenshots and verified cost categories carry more weight than marketing claims.
3. Make motion feel directional: route movement, light, and pacing support the logistics story without competing with copy.
4. Treat every number as business-critical: strong hierarchy, tabular numerals, and calm contrast make values easy to scan.
5. Keep trust visible: no account, local data, public legal links, and accurate store availability remain easy to verify.
6. Publish for a defined transport-professional problem, not page-count growth. Dates change only after meaningful review.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for contrast and interaction. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic landmarks, descriptive image text, 44 px touch targets, reduced-motion preferences, and layouts from narrow mobile screens through large desktop viewports. Italian is primary; content architecture keeps English localization separate and ready for expansion.
